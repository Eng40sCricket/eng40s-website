Selection Standards

To be considered for England Over 40s, players should be playing regular Premier League cricket or an equivalent standard, or have a recent strong track record in Minor Counties or County Cricket.

Players should be able to demonstrate consistently high standards through their Play-Cricket record, with particular emphasis on performances over the previous 2 to 3 years.

A good level of general fitness, strong fielding standards, and the ability to meet the all-round demands of representative cricket are also expected.

Meeting these standards does not in itself guarantee selection, but they form an important part of the overall assessment process.
