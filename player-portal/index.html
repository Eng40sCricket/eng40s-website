Apply to represent England Over 40s Cricket as a player, umpire, scorer, or match official.

Player Application

For cricketers seeking selection consideration for England Over 40s.

  • Playing regular Premier League cricket or equivalent standard
  • Strong Play-Cricket record over the previous 2 to 3 years
  • Good general fitness and fielding standards
  • Ability to meet the all-round demands of representative cricket

Meeting these standards does not guarantee selection, but they form an important part of the assessment process.

Officials Application

For umpires, scorers, and other match officials with strong experience.

  • Umpires officiating regularly at Premier League level or above
  • Scorers experienced in electronic scoring including PCS Pro
  • Understanding of NV Play streaming and keying requirements
  • Volunteer-led organisation with expenses at Premier League rates

Reasonable expenses or remuneration may be available in line with typical Premier League rates, depending on role.
