Players, Volunteers & Officials

Apply to represent England Over 40s Cricket as a player, or to support the team as a volunteer, umpire or scorer.

Player Application

For cricketers seeking selection consideration for England Over 40s.

  • Playing regular Premier League cricket or equivalent standard
  • Strong Play-Cricket record over the previous 2 to 3 years
  • Good general fitness and fielding standards
  • Ability to meet the all-round demands of representative cricket

Meeting these standards does not guarantee selection, but they form an important part of the assessment process.

Volunteers & Officials

For volunteers who can help run the team, and for experienced umpires and scorers.

  • Committee roles in performance, commercial, media and operations
  • Tour managers, assistant managers and NV Play live streaming
  • Umpires at Premier League level and scorers experienced in PCS Pro
  • Qualified accountants and legal professionals as advisers

Committee and adviser roles are voluntary. Match officials may receive reasonable expenses or remuneration in line with typical Premier League rates, depending on role.
