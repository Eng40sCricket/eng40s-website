Officials Application — England Over 40s Cricket

We welcome applications from suitably experienced officials, including umpires and scorers, who can demonstrate high standards at strong club level and above.

Umpires should normally be officiating regularly in Premier League cricket or at a comparable standard or higher.

Scorers should be experienced and confident in electronic scoring, including PCS Pro, and should understand the keying requirements involved when scoring alongside NV Play streaming so that correct ball clips can be captured for highlights purposes.

England Over 40s Cricket is a volunteer-led organisation. However, reasonable expenses or remuneration may be available in line with typical Premier League rates, depending on role and appointment.

Availability & Supporting Statement

England Over 40s Cricket is a volunteer-led, not-for-profit organisation. Reasonable expenses or remuneration may be available broadly in line with usual Premier League rates, depending on the role and appointment. This will be discussed individually with successful applicants.
