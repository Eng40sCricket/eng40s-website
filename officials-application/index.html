Volunteer With England Over 40s Cricket

England Over 40s Cricket has grown quickly, and we are building a wider volunteer team so that the organisation does not rely on a small number of people. We welcome interest from players, former players and anyone in the cricket community with skills to offer, including club chairmen, secretaries, scorers and professionals who love the game.

Some roles are year-round, others are a few days a season or a single tour. Each area has a lead who helps coordinate it, and you are welcome to put yourself forward for a lead role.

Committee, subcommittee and adviser roles are voluntary. For match officials, reasonable expenses or remuneration may be available in line with typical Premier League rates, depending on role and appointment.

Where You Could Help

Performance

  • Coaching support for the Lions and, in time, the main squad
  • Player assessment: watching matches and reporting on form
  • Selection for the Lions and possibly the main squad

Commercial

  • Sponsorship and partnerships: finding and looking after commercial partners
  • Marketing: promoting the team to new audiences

Media & Communications

  • Social media management across Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Threads and YouTube
  • Content creation: match photography, video and highlights editing, player features, graphics and match reports

Operations

  • Tour managers and assistant managers for overseas tours and major tournaments
  • Live streaming of matches to YouTube using NV Play

Match Officials

  • Umpires officiating regularly in Premier League cricket or at a comparable standard or higher
  • Scorers experienced and confident in electronic scoring, including PCS Pro
  • Scorers who understand the keying requirements when scoring alongside NV Play streaming, so that correct ball clips are captured for highlights

Professional Advisers

  • Qualified accountants (ACA, ACCA, ICAS or CTA) willing to support our Treasurer with budgets, tour costings and annual accounts
  • Legal professionals able to help with contract and sponsorship agreement reviews, insurance matters and governance
