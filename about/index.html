About England Over 40s Cricket

Our mission, values, and commitment to masters cricket.

Our Mission

England Over 40's Cricket is a voluntary, non-profit making organisation, self-funded by its recreational players to continue to engage cricketers above the age of 40yrs to play in veterans matches and tournaments at an international level, whilst at the same time promoting physical and mental well-being through sport and team sociability. It does so under endorsement from the England and Wales cricket board (ECB).

Organisational Overview

England Over 40s Cricket Limited is dedicated to fostering high-standard recreational cricket for players over the age of 40. Endorsed by the ECB, we provide opportunities for competitive play both in England and on overseas tours, including participation in prestigious events like the IMC Over 40s World Cup. Our focus extends beyond the field, emphasizing the physical and mental well-being of our players through sport and strong team camaraderie.

Our Values & Purpose

  • Passion for Cricket

    We are driven by a deep love for the game, promoting competitive spirit and enjoyment among veteran players.

  • Well-being

    We prioritize the physical and mental health of our players, recognizing the positive impact of sport and social engagement.

  • Camaraderie

    Building strong team bonds and fostering a supportive community is central to our ethos.

  • Excellence

    We strive for high standards in play and organisation, representing England with pride at an international level.
