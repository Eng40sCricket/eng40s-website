Why sponsor England Over 40s Cricket

A partnership platform with reach and relevance.

Sponsors gain meaningful exposure, a credible setting, and practical activation opportunities across the wider programme.

Measurable reach

  • More than four million views across Facebook and Instagram in a season.
  • All of it organic: nothing boosted, nothing bought.
  • 2,000–3,500 views for each streamed match, rising to almost 7,000 for a World Cup semi-final.
  • 19 matches streamed live in a season, with commentary, graphics and replays.
  • 66,000 people reached in a single day at our peak.

Premium association

  • England representation in high-standard ODI and T20 over 40s cricket.
  • A premium setting shaped by heritage, competition, and hospitality.
  • Nine in ten followers are men, and six in ten are aged 35 to 54.
  • Strong relevance for finance, travel, lifestyle, service, and premium consumer brands.

Activation options

  • Branding across live streams, social, and matchday settings.
  • Commentary mentions, branded features, and digital integration.
  • VIP hospitality for clients, guests, and stakeholders.
  • Player access and behind-the-scenes content opportunities.
  • Photography and video assets that extend value beyond matchday.

Partnership levels

Four sponsorship levels. One clear route in.

Premium Partner, Gold Partner, Silver Partner, and Zing Stumps Partner give brands a straightforward route from platform-wide visibility to distinctive in-play activation.

Lead position

Premium Partner

Lead-level visibility across the full England Over 40s Cricket platform.

  • A two-year partnership.
  • Lead position on the playing shirt.
  • Training kit branding.
  • Logo on all live streams.
  • Prime ground signage at all matches.
  • Regular social media features and credits.
  • Trophy and award recognition.
  • Behind-the-scenes content access.
  • Lead listing on the website, with sector exclusivity.
  • End-of-season report.
Ideal for

National brands, financial services businesses, and major corporations.

Sponsorship tier

Gold Partner

A strong multi-channel partnership with broad event and digital visibility.

  • Training kit branding.
  • Logo on selected live streams.
  • Ground signage at major tournaments.
  • Regular social media credits.
  • Website listing.
  • Sector exclusivity by agreement.
  • End-of-season report.
Ideal for

Regional enterprises and ambitious growing brands.

Sponsorship tier

Silver Partner

A smart entry point for brands seeking credible exposure and event presence.

  • A playing-shirt sleeve position, subject to availability.
  • Ground signage at selected matches.
  • Monthly social media mentions and match graphics.
  • Website listing.
  • Sector exclusivity by agreement.
  • End-of-season report.
Ideal for

Local businesses and service providers.

Sponsorship tier

Zing Stumps Partner

A distinctive in-play partnership with repeated visual impact.

  • Your branding on the Zing stumps at all matches.
  • In shot for every ball of the live stream.
  • Credits in match highlights.
  • Website listing.
  • Sole partner in this position.
  • End-of-season report.
Ideal for

Tech brands, equipment suppliers, and brands seeking standout matchday visibility.

Every partnership is tailored. Hospitality, player appearances and business networking can be built into any partnership by arrangement, and investment levels are available on request.
