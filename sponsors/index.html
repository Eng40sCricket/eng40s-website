Partnership levels

Four sponsorship levels. One clear route in.

Premium Partner, Gold Partner, Silver Partner, and Zing Stumps Partner give brands a straightforward route from platform-wide visibility to distinctive in-play activation.

Lead position

Premium Partner

Lead-level visibility across the full England Over-40s Cricket platform.

  • Prime on-ground signage at all matches.
  • Logo overlay on all live broadcasts.
  • Team jersey and training kit branding.
  • Full Triple Crown hospitality with VIP seating.
  • Two to three player meet-and-greets annually.
  • Daily social media mentions.
  • Trophy and presentation recognition.
  • Exclusive behind-the-scenes content.
Ideal for

National brands, financial services businesses, and major corporations.

Sponsorship tier

Gold Partner

A strong multi-channel partnership with broad event and digital visibility.

  • Prominent signage at major tournaments.
  • Logo presence on select live broadcasts.
  • Training kit branding.
  • VIP access across two days plus four event passes.
  • Weekly social media features.
  • One to two player meet-and-greets.
  • Professional photography and video assets.
Ideal for

Regional enterprises and ambitious growing brands.

Sponsorship tier

Silver Partner

A smart entry point for brands seeking credible exposure and event presence.

  • Company branding at select matches.
  • Social media graphics and promotions.
  • Two event passes plus one day of hospitality.
  • Monthly social mentions.
  • Website recognition.
  • Access to event photography.
Ideal for

Local businesses and service providers.

Sponsorship tier

Zing Stumps Partner

A distinctive in-play partnership with repeated visual impact.

  • Custom branding on all Zing stumps.
  • High-frequency visibility in every match.
  • Prominent presence in live broadcasts and highlights.
  • A unique, exclusive sponsorship opportunity.
  • Natural brand integration during play.
Ideal for

Tech brands, equipment suppliers, and brands seeking standout matchday visibility.
