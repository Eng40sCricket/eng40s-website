London, United Kingdom — Wednesday 25 March 2026

England Over 40s Cricket has announced its squad for the IMC O40s World Cup 2026, to be held in Georgetown, Guyana, from 17–31 October 2026.

The England party will be led on the field by captain Darren Stevens, the former professional cricketer, alongside a strong group of high-quality club players drawn from across the country. The squad has been selected from a wider national programme, reflecting form, commitment and suitability for international tournament cricket.

"Today's announcement is a hugely exciting moment for everyone involved with England Over 40s Cricket. To represent England at a World Cup is a privilege, and we are proud of the standard of players who have earned selection. With Darren's leadership and the depth of quality in the group, we are looking forward to competing strongly in Guyana."

— Chris Mays, Director and Tour Manager

"I'm really excited to be leading the squad into the 2026 season. We have plenty of games through the year to help us build towards the World Cup in Guyana. We've built a very exciting squad over the last two years. We lost in the semi-final last time out, so we'll be doing our best to go one step further."

— Darren Stevens, Captain

The tournament is a major milestone in England Over 40s Cricket's international calendar and follows England's semi-final appearance at the last edition. It also reflects the organisation's mission to provide competitive, high-quality international cricket for players aged 40+ in an ECB-endorsed environment.
