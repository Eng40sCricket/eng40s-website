England Over 40s Cricket — 26 June 2026

England Over 40s Cricket and The Forty Club (XL) have confirmed an informal development understanding — a shared commitment to keeping the Spirit of Cricket alive for players aged 40 and above.

The Forty Club is an open-age wandering club with no strict 40+ rule, though many of its members are 35 and over. It offers a strong national fixture list and a long-standing commitment to the Spirit of Cricket, making it a natural environment for players approaching or in their 40s who want to continue playing at a high standard.

What the understanding means

  • Players aged 40+ (and those approaching 40) can join The Forty Club as normal members.
  • XL regional managers will maintain informal awareness of England-qualified and approaching-40 players who demonstrate outstanding performance, fitness, and attitude.
  • A small number of these players may be quietly recommended to England Over 40s for trial or development opportunities.

Both organisations remain fully independent. This is not a formal selection pathway or a guarantee of progression — England Over 40s selection will continue to be based on performances across all environments, including club cricket, county veterans cricket, The Forty Club, and elsewhere.

For more information about The Forty Club visit fortyclub.com. — England Over 40s Cricket
