England Over 40s Cricket — 24 July 2026

England Over 40s Cricket have named a 14-man squad for the Triple Crown 2026, to be played at Bishop's Stortford Cricket Club, Hertfordshire from 28–30 July 2026.

Darren Stevens captains the side, with Sean Park as vice-captain. England enter the tournament as defending champions after a clean sweep of all three matches at Stirling in 2025, and will face Ireland, Scotland and Wales across the three days.
